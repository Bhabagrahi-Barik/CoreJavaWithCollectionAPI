==================================== Abstraction Vs Encapsulation ====================================

1) Encapsulation is the process of hiding data and implementation details from the outside worlds. It encapsulates/bundles data and the implementation details so the unauthorized access of outside world can't change it's internal state.

Where as the Abstraction is the process of hiding complexities at design level. It focuses on what an object can do rether that how it does it.

2) Abstraction focuses on Hiding complexity at Design level whereas	Encapsulation focuses on Hiding data and state at Implementation level .

3)Abstraction focuses on What an object does whereas Encapsulation focuses on How data is kept safe and structured .

4) Abstraction in java is achieved through interface and abstract keyword, whereas encapsulation is achieved through private keyword and access those private fields through public getter and setter methods.

5) Example of Abstraction:- A TV Remote: You press the power button (what it does) without needing to know the infrared circuit board layout (how it works).

Example of Encapsulation:- A Capsule / Safe Box: Your sensitive data (like a bank balance) is locked inside a secure container, and you can only interact with it through controlled methods (like deposit() or withdraw()).
